# v0.2.0

*Release date: August 12, 2026*

---

## Added

- **In-conversation quizzes.** The tutor can now quiz you mid-conversation
  without breaking your place in it - questions appear in a side drawer,
  either multiple-choice or drag-and-drop word bank. A quiz picks up right
  where you left off if you reconnect mid-quiz, and anything you get wrong
  feeds a per-conversation spaced-repetition list, so those words are more
  likely to come back around in a future quiz.
- **Update notifications.** A bell notification icon in the top bar (visible on every
  page, not just mid-conversation) now lets you know when a new app
  version or a refreshed avatar/voice/photo library is available. Click
  the bell, then the notification, for details and a one-click
  **Update & Relaunch** - or **Update Assets** if it's only the avatar/
  voice library that changed, which doesn't need a restart. You can also
  check manually any time from the profile menu or Settings -> Updates.
